# V20+ family — design log toward combined5 SOTA

Target: unified architecture on combined5 (etoile + ZJU 5-BS + SUTD + florence + munich, 120k samples) that approaches per-cr SOTA across cr=4/8/16. Constraint: FLOPs ≤ v9u, keep v9u's ComplexLowRankEncoder + HybridRankDecoder core, retain *some* dilated conv.

## Current scoreboard (combined5)

| Model | cr=4 | cr=8 | cr=16 | Notes |
|----|----|----|----|----|
| v9u (baseline) | −20.05 | −16.26 | −13.39 | DCREncoderBlock 7-conv dilated + ComplexLowRank + Hybrid |
| v20 (FCI cascade) | **−20.15** | **−16.77** | −13.07 | 3-conv 3x3→1x9→9x1, SA, hsigmoid |
| TransNet | −18.80 | −16.95 | **−15.11** | Global MHA |

| v12_rX | **−20.24** | — | — | Larger decoder out branch |
| **per-cr SOTA target** | **−20.24** | **−18.03** | **−15.11** | |

The gap to close: cr=4 by ~0.1, cr=8 by ~1.3, cr=16 by ~2 dB. cr=16 is the hardest because attention dominates that regime.

## Prior experiments — what we learned

| Exp | Result | Lesson |
|----|----|----|
| v5_complex (lowrank only, complex bilinear) | worse than v5 baseline | Complex encoder alone is insufficient — v9u's gain over v5 came from decoder/FiLM, not just complex |
| v20 (FCI replacing DCREncoderBlock) | cr=4/8 +0.1 to +0.5 dB, **cr=16 −0.3 dB** | Long solid 1×9 / 9×1 kernels good at low cr; lose multi-scale benefit at high cr |
| v21a (dilated-front + FCI + FiLM-from-z refine) | cr=4 close to v20, **cr=16 stuck at −11.6** | Dilated front + FCI + FiLM together insufficient for cr=16 |
| v21b (bilinear projection in dilate path) | slow warmup, cr=16 −12.3 | Replacing Linear(2048, code_dim) with bilinear didn't recover cr=16 |
| v22 (no dilate path, lowrank only) | all cr regress vs v9u (cr=4 −3.8, cr=16 −1.1) | Dilate path is genuinely useful, especially at low cr |
| v23 (v9u + 16-tok 1-MHA branch) | cr=4/8 slightly behind v9u, **cr=16 catches v9u same-ep** | Tiny attention helps cr=16 but distracts from cr=4/8 |

**Diagnosis for cr=16**: ComplexLowRank (separable bilinear) + dilated conv (local) cannot express arbitrary-position pair correlations. Attention can. cr=16's tight codeword (128 dim) makes this gap manifest.

## v25 — design decisions

Final architecture (one file: `models/dcrnet_v25.py`):

**Encoder**:
1. `z_a = ComplexLowRankEncoder(r_enc=512)` — v9u core, unchanged.
2. `z_b = HybridDilateFCIPath(x)`: replaces v9u's DCREncoderBlock + projection. Specifically:
   - `Conv3x1(d=2) → LReLU` — keep one small dilated conv for cheap multi-scale (lesson from v22: dilate is needed)
   - `Conv1x9(d=1) → LReLU` — FCI long horizontal (lesson from v20)
   - `Conv9x1(d=1)` — FCI long vertical
   - SpatialGate (3×3, identity-init) — cheap data-dependent emphasis
   - `Linear(2048, code_dim) → LayerNorm → gate`
3. `z_c = TinyAttnBranchSlim(x)` — d_model=16, n_heads=4, 16 tokens (patch=8). Zero-init projection so it contributes nothing at step 0. **This is the targeted cr=16 fix.**
4. Fusion: `z_a` and `z_b` go through existing `EncoderFuseMLP(64)` (v9u's). `z_c` added on top with its own gate.

**Decoder**:
- HybridRankDecoder (v9u, unchanged)
- IterativeRefineZ: refine stages with FiLM from z (lesson from v21a). Cheap insurance even if late-stage gain is marginal.
- hsigmoid output (v20 lesson — free improvement).

## FLOPs budget (cr=4 baseline)

| Component | v9u | v25 | Δ |
|----|----|----|----|
| ComplexLowRankEncoder | ~2.1M | ~2.1M | 0 |
| enc_dilate conv | 117K | ~90K | **−27K** (3 conv vs 7) |
| Linear projection (2048→512) | 1.05M | 1.05M | 0 |
| EncoderFuseMLP | small | small | 0 |
| TinyAttn (new) | 0 | ~45K | **+45K** |
| HybridRankDecoder | ~1.0M | ~1.0M | 0 |
| Refine + FiLM | small | +5K | **+5K** |
| Net cr=4 | 4.36M | ~4.34M | **−20K** |
| Net cr=16 | 1.43M | ~1.41M | **−20K** |

Within v9u budget.

## Decision rationale

- Why hybrid 3-conv block (1 dilated + 2 long): lose 4 of v9u's 7 conv layers (saves FLOPs + simplifies gradient flow on multi-BS data), keep enough dilation for cr=16 compression.
- Why slim TinyAttn (d_model=16 not 32): v23 used d_model=32 (+80K MACs). Slim version is half the cost; whether 16-dim head capacity is enough is the experimental question. If it underperforms, escalate.
- Why FiLM refine: cheap, principled (z has info beyond rank-1 outer products), v21a showed it doesn't hurt. Insurance.
- Why hsigmoid: v20 confirmed strict improvement, no cost.

### v26 — clean 2-branch redesign (2026-05-25 07:45)

User feedback: v25/v25c stacked too many components (v9u DCREncoderBlock + Linear + parallel attention + FiLM refine = 3 effective encoder paths and a beefed decoder). Return to fundamentals.

**v26 architecture (2 encoder branches only)**:
- z_a = ComplexLowRankEncoder(x)                    [main: low-rank prior]
- z_b = DilatedAttnPath(x)                          [supplement: SERIAL dilated -> attn]
    - Conv3x1(d=2) + Conv1x3(d=3) + Conv3x3(d=1)    [multi-scale local]
    - PatchEmbed(2 -> d_model=24, 8x8 patches)      [16 tokens]
    - 1x Transformer block (4 heads, FFN=48)        [global pairs]
    - Mean pool + Linear(24, code_dim)              [project to z_b]
- z = MLP-fuse(z_a, z_b)
- Decoder: HybridRankDecoder + plain IterativeRefine + hsigmoid (NO FiLM)

**Why this beats v25c stack**: Replaces v9u's DilateEncoderPath (DCREncoderBlock + Linear(2048,code_dim)) — the Linear was the dominant cost (~1M MACs at cr=4). PatchEmbed + small attention + Linear(24,code_dim) is much cheaper. We REDIRECT the saved FLOPs into the attention mechanism that's known to help at cr=16.

**FLOPs vs v9u** (einsum-corrected, real cost):
| CR | v9u | v26 | Δ FLOPs | Δ params |
|----|----|----|----|----|
| 4 | 6.749M | **5.704M** | **−15.5%** | −1.03M (−26%) |
| 8 | 4.795M | **4.268M** | −11.0% | −0.51M (−25%) |
| 16 | 3.818M | **3.549M** | −7.0% | −0.25M (−23%) |

Strict reduction in both. Launched v26 cr=4/8/16 with v9u recipe (lr=1e-3 cr=4, lr=2e-3 cr=8/16).

### v27 — keep v9u dilate path, insert attention residual (2026-05-25 08:10)

Same 2-branch contract, but supplement attention is now an *enhancement* of v9u's conv features, not a replacement of the compressor:

z_b = DilateAttnEncoderPath(x) =

### v28 — TransNet+CLNet inspired full redesign (2026-05-25 08:18)

User feedback: "Linear太重 整个dilate path设计不合理 参考transnet和clnet重新设计".

Diagnosis of v9u DilateEncoderPath: 7 small (2->2) convs total ~250 params + Linear(2048, code_dim) ~1M MACs. The conv stack is anemic (each layer only 36 params), and Linear is doing all the heavy lifting on weak features.

Solutions from TransNet/CLNet:
- TransNet: 2x Transformer over (32 tokens × 64 dim) THEN dense Linear. Heavy linear works *because* attention pre-mixes features.
- CLNet: 2-branch (long-kernel + channel expansion) → concat → Conv1d. Rich features before compression.

v28 z_b = TransLitePath:
- Conv3x3 + Conv1x9 + Conv9x1                    [CLNet FCI cascade]
- Reshape -> (B, 32 row-tokens, 64 dim)         [TransNet token-view]
- Linear(64, d_attn=24) bottleneck              [cheap mixing prep]
- 1 Transformer block (d=24, h=4, FFN=48)       [global token mix]
- per-token Linear(24, m=8) -> (B, 256)         [structured local extract]
- Linear(256, code_dim)                          [small dense compress]
- LN + gate
